Automatically scans the full 24-hour IST day for liquid Binance USDT perpetuals. It finds each coin's strongest continuous negative window and strongest continuous positive window, then ranks the results by average return across that window. No manual start/end hour selection required.

Automatic ranking: the scanner checks all possible consecutive windows across the whole day, including windows that cross midnight (for example, 22:00–02:00 IST). Trading Coins — Top 10 Long & Short Candidates

Scans Binance USDT perpetual contracts with the highest 24-hour quote volume, then ranks them by a simple momentum score using 24h change, recent 1h change, and 15m change.

How ranking works: candidates must be USDT perpetuals and pass the volume filter. Momentum score = 40% of 24h percentage change + 40% of last 1h candle return + 20% of last 15m candle return. Long candidates rank highest; short candidates rank lowest. This is a momentum screen, not a complete trading strategy. Confirm trend, support/resistance, funding, open interest, volatility, and risk before any trade. No trades are executed.
