30-day historical hour-slot analysis · IST

Next 2 / 3 / 4 Hours — Positive & Negative Coins

Scans the top 200 eligible Binance USDT perpetual markets by 24-hour quote volume, then compares the upcoming IST hour slots with the same hour slots over the last 30 days. It lists coins whose average return is consistently positive or consistently negative across your selected 2, 3, or 4-hour window.

How this works: each selected upcoming hour is compared with that same IST hour's average hourly candle return over the last 30 days. A coin is labelled consistently positive only if every selected hour's historical average is above zero; consistently negative only if every selected hour's average is below zero. These are historical tendencies, not predictions or guaranteed trade signals. The scan uses the current IST hour slot as the starting slot.

Market scanner

Trading Coins — Top 10 Long & Short Candidates

Scans Binance USDT perpetual contracts with the highest 24-hour quote volume, then ranks them by a simple momentum score using 24h change, recent 1h change, and 15m change.

How ranking works: candidates must be USDT perpetuals and pass the volume filter. Momentum score = 40% of 24h percentage change + 40% of last 1h candle return + 20% of last 15m candle return. Long candidates rank highest; short candidates rank lowest. This is a momentum screen, not a complete trading strategy. Confirm trend, support/resistance, funding, open interest, volatility, and risk before any trade. No trades are executed.
